Condition the Dog

You're running a classical conditioning experiment. Give your dog a name, then use the bell and the food bowl to see how a brand-new response can be trained into existence.

The idea Right now, the bell means nothing to your dog. Food, on the other hand, automatically makes any dog drool — no training needed. Ring the bell, and a short timer starts — get food to your dog before it runs out and that counts as pairing them. Wait too long, and nothing is learned that time. Your goal: get the bell alone to start making your dog drool.
