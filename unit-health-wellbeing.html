Health and Well-Being

Stress, one mental health disorder studied in depth, treatment and prevention, and a health problem examined through changing or differing prevalence — built around IB Psychology's Health and Well-Being option.

Ontario Crosswalk (HSP3U / HSB4U) This unit's activities satisfy Strand A (Research and Inquiry Skills) through embedded skills — evaluating bias in etiology models, analyzing correlational vs. experimental evidence in treatment studies — rather than through topic-by-topic mapping. Content-specific strand alignment is added as each topic is finalized.
