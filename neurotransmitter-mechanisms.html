SSRI / SNRI

  • First-line treatment for SAD
  • Gradual onset — benefit builds over 8–12 weeks
  • ~50–60% of people respond
  • No addiction or dependence risk

Benzodiazepine

  • Second-line — used when SSRIs haven't worked
  • Fast onset — improvement possible within one week
  • ~78% respond in clinical trials
  • Risk of tolerance, dependence, withdrawal
