Key Concepts: Classical Conditioning

Unconditioned Stimulus UCS
Something that naturally and automatically triggers a response, with no learning required.
Unconditioned Response UCR
The automatic, unlearned reaction triggered by the UCS.
Conditioned Stimulus CS
Something that used to mean nothing, but through repeated pairing with a UCS, has learned to trigger a response on its own.
Conditioned Response CR
The learned reaction triggered by the CS — usually similar to the UCR, but now happening in response to something that originally meant nothing.
