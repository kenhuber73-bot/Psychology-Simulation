History of Psychology · Psychoanalysis · Lesson 1

An Introduction to Psychoanalysis

How a Viennese neurologist studying hysteria arrived at a whole theory of mind — from Freud's own neurological research and the Oedipus complex, through defense mechanisms and dream interpretation, to what a 2014 Cochrane review actually found when psychoanalysis got tested against a control group.

01

Hook

Someone tells you about a dream they had last night. Do you assume it means nothing — just neurons firing at random — or that it means something, even if they can't say what?

Freud built an entire theory of the mind on the second answer. Today you'll interpret a dream the way he did, before finding out whether his method holds up.

→ The deck below opens with a real recorded dream ("Irma's Dream") — read it with your group and try interpreting it before Freud tells you what he thought it meant.
