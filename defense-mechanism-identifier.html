Defense Mechanism Identifier

This activity is modeled on the methodology researchers actually use to study defense mechanisms — Bond's Defense Style Questionnaire (DSQ). Real DSQ research scores each defense as the average of several separate statements, then ranks a person's defenses against each other to see which ones stand out as characteristic. This version uses original items written for the 14 defenses from this unit — not the real DSQ's actual questions or scoring norms — but the same underlying logic.

How it works

You'll rate 42 statements from 1 (strongly disagree) to 9 (strongly agree) — three statements per defense, for a more reliable read than a single question could give. Your results identify which specific defenses you recognized most in yourself, ranked from highest to lowest, then grouped into the Primitive / Secondary / Mature tiers from class. It takes about 12–14 minutes.

This is an original reflection tool built for classroom use, written to illustrate the defense mechanisms covered in this unit — it is not a validated psychological test, not a clinical or diagnostic instrument, and your answers are not saved or sent anywhere. Your scores are ipsative — they show which defenses stood out relative to your own other answers, not compared to other students. Everyone uses defense mechanisms; recognizing a Primitive one in yourself here doesn't mean anything is wrong with you.
