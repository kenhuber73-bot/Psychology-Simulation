The Case File

In the early 1900s, Carl Jung gave patients a list of words and asked them to respond with the first word that came to mind, timing each response with a stopwatch. He believed a slow response, an unusual response, repeating the stimulus word back, giving the same "safe" response to several different words, or later forgetting what you'd said, could all signal that a word had touched an unconscious emotional cluster he called a complex.

Here's your run. Rows with more than one flag are marked as a possible complex indicator — worth your own scrutiny, not a diagnosis.
