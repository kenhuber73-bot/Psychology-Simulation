The Stroop Task

You'll see a series of color words, each printed in an ink color. Your job: click the button matching the ink color — not what the word says.

Example If the word "BLUE" appears printed in red ink, click the red button — the word says "blue," but the ink is red.

20 trials in two blocks · go as fast as you can while staying accurate

Block 2

Heads up The next 10 trials are trickier — the ink color won't always match the word. Keep going as fast and accurately as you can.
