The Personality Pattern Inventory

A 72-item self-report questionnaire modeled on the nine classic clinical scales popularized by the Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory (MMPI) — rebuilt here with original items for classroom use.

You'll read 72 short statements and say how much each one sounds like you. It takes about 8–10 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers — answer with your first instinct rather than overthinking it.

At the end you'll get a profile across nine personality & adjustment scales, shown as a chart you can discuss or compare with classmates.

NOT A DIAGNOSTIC TOOL. This is an original, educational adaptation inspired by MMPI-style scale concepts — it is not the MMPI, not the IDR-MPT, and not clinically validated. It's built for teaching how self-report personality inventories work, not for assessing or diagnosing anyone. If something you notice in yourself is worrying you, talk to a school counselor, doctor, or another trusted adult.

Higher isn't "worse" — each scale reflects a tendency, and everyone scores somewhere.

Remember: this is a self-report instrument you completed once, on your own, with no clinical scoring or validity checks behind it. Real personality assessment involves trained interpretation, context, and usually more than one measure. Treat this as a conversation-starter about how these instruments work, not a verdict on who you are.
