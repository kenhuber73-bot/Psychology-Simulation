Psych Simulations · Research-Based Task

Scrambled Sentences

You'll see 6 words, shuffled out of order. Tap 5 of them — in order — to build a grammatically correct sentence. One word won't be used.

Example Words: my joke was pretty funny lame
Two different 5-word sentences are possible here, depending on which word you leave out. Both are grammatically fine — go with whichever comes to mind first. If you tap a combination that isn't a valid sentence, you'll be told and the words will reset so you can try again.

This task is modeled on the Scrambled Sentences Task used in interpretation-bias research (the sentences here are original, not the published research stimuli). The idea: every one of these sentences was genuinely ambiguous — either completion was equally grammatical. Which one your mind reached for first is the "interpretation." Research on social anxiety has found this measure correlates fairly strongly with self-reported anxiety symptoms — worth comparing against your Social Situations Reflection or SIAS score if you tried those. This is a classroom demonstration, not a diagnostic tool, and nothing is saved.
